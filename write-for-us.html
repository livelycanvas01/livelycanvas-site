Before you commit

What we ask of contributors

1

One post to start

No standing commitment required. Most contributors start with a single piece — we'll talk about more if it's a good fit on both sides.

2

Original & unpublished

Your piece needs to be new — not previously posted on your own blog, Medium, or anywhere else, before or after it runs here.

3

1,200–2,000 words

Specific and practical, written in first person where it fits. We'll edit for clarity but the voice should stay yours.

What's in it for you

What you get

  • A full byline and a short author bio with one link back to your site or social profile
  • Your piece shared through our newsletter and Instagram at publication
  • Ongoing exposure to a growing, engaged household-focused readership
  • Backlink opportunities — genuinely useful for your own site's SEO
  • A shot at becoming a regular contributor if the fit is right
  • Paid sponsored placements available separately, on request
Editorial guidelines

What we will and won't publish

We publish

  • Specific, practical stories with a clear, usable takeaway — not generic advice
  • Family-friendly tone and content throughout
  • Pieces that cite a source for any statistical or medical claim

We don't publish

  • Pure product roundups written mainly to place affiliate links
  • Paid or sponsored placements without clear disclosure — we always mark these if agreed in advance
  • AI-generated drafts submitted as finished work — use whatever tools help you research or edit, but the piece needs a real point of view behind it

How to pitch

Skip the full draft for now — send a short pitch first so we can give feedback before you write.

Include:

  • — A working title
  • — 2–3 sentences on the story and its takeaway
  • — Which category it fits
  • — A link to something else you've written, if you have one
Email your pitch

What happens next

We read every pitch and reply within two weeks. If it's a fit, we'll ask for a full draft with a rough deadline. If you haven't heard back in two weeks, a follow-up is welcome.
